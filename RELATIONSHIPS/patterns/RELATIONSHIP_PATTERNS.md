# Identified Relationship Patterns: Sarah

---

## Pattern 1: The Unavailable Man

### Description
Repeatedly choosing partners who are emotionally or literally unavailable.

### Evidence
| Partner | Type of Unavailability |
|---------|----------------------|
| Sebas | Literally unavailable (pursuing another woman) |
| NM | Emotionally unavailable (controlling, distant) |

### Why This Happens
- Familiar dynamic (examine family patterns)
- Challenge = love (if it's hard, it must be real)
- Avoiding true intimacy by choosing impossible situations
- Trying to "win" unavailable love (repetition compulsion)

### The Breakthrough
**Ivan** — Available, present, reciprocal. Proves she CAN choose differently.

---

## Pattern 2: Over-Giving to Prevent Abandonment

### Description
Excessive emotional/sexual/practical investment hoping it will make them stay.

### Evidence
- Gave everything to Sebas → still left
- Censored herself for NM → still problematic
- Pattern of exhausting herself for partners

### Why It Doesn't Work
- Can't "earn" someone's love through labor
- Creates imbalance that breeds resentment
- Attracts people who take without giving
- Loses herself in the process

### Healthy Alternative
Reciprocity. Match investment to what you receive. (See: Ivan dynamic)

## Pattern 3: Christmas as Breaking Point

### Description
Relationships ending or reaching crisis during holiday season.

### Evidence
- 2021: Sebas betrayal revealed December
- 2025-26: NM relationship deteriorated around holidays

### Why December
- Heightened vulnerability during family time
- Expectations of love contrast with reality
- Partners may feel trapped and act out
- OR partners reveal true colors when pressure increases

### Management
See [Christmas Abandonment Wound](../wounds/01_CHRISTMAS_ABANDONMENT.md)

## Pattern 4: The Self-Blame Default

### Description
Automatically assuming relationship problems are her fault.

### Evidence
- *"¿Qué hice mal?"* as first response
- Apologizing for existing (NM relationship)
- Internalizing partners' failures as her inadequacy

### Why This Happens
- Cultural conditioning (women responsible for relationships)
- Possibly learned in family
- Illusion of control (if it's my fault, I can fix it)
- Avoids the helplessness of truth (they chose badly, not me)

### Reframe
Their behavior reflects THEIR character, not your worth.

---

## Pattern 5: Loss of Self in Relationships

### Description
Adapting personality, censoring expression, becoming what partner wants.

### Evidence
- Walking on eggshells with NM
- Filtering herself to avoid punishment
- Not knowing who she is outside of partnership

### Why This Happens
- Anxious attachment — will do anything to maintain bond
- "Not enough" wound — must become "better" version
- Trauma response — safety through compliance

### Recovery
The Ivan relationship shows her authentic self IS loveable without modification.

---

## The Positive Pattern: Capacity for Health

### Evidence
- Built genuine trust with Ivan in 1 month
- Recognizes quality when she sees it (*"10/10"*, *"husband material"*)
- Can articulate needs clearly when safe
- Maintains friendships (shows relational capacity)

### Implication
She's not broken. She's been choosing from a limited pool. When a healthy option appears, she responds healthily.
