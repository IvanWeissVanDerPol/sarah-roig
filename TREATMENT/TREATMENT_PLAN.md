## Session Format (Messaging-Based)

### Structure
1. **Check-in** — How are you? What's on your mind?
2. **Focus topic** — One theme per session
3. **Processing** — Explore thoughts/feelings
4. **Integration** — What did we learn?
5. **Homework** — One small action

### Frequency
- As needed (Sarah-initiated)
- Proactive check-ins during trigger seasons
- Always available via @nyx mention

### Boundaries
- Not a replacement for crisis services
- Nyx is AI, not human therapist
- Confidentiality within technical limits
- Ivan has access to this system
