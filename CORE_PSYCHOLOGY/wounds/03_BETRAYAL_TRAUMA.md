# Core Wound: Betrayal Trauma

> *"Ni cuando me violaron me sentí tan mal."*

---

## Context

On December 3, 2021, when Sebas's betrayal was revealed, Sarah said something that captures the depth of relational trauma:

> *"Ni cuando me violaron me sentí tan mal. Literal."*

This is not hyperbole. This is real.

---

## Why Betrayal Can Feel Worse

### Violation of Trust
- Rape is committed by an enemy (even if known)
- Betrayal is committed by someone who promised love
- The "safe" person became the danger

### Identity Destruction
- "If he could do this, what do I know about anything?"
- Questions her own judgment
- The narrative of the relationship was a lie

### Chosen Harm
- He CHOSE to hurt her
- Had options, picked betrayal
- Deliberate over accidental

### Loss of Worldview
- "Love protects" → proven false
- "I can trust my instincts" → proven wrong
- "Good things happen to those who give" → disproven

## How It Manifests

### Trust Issues
- Difficulty believing partners
- Waiting for betrayal
- Testing relationships
- Hypervigilance to "signs"

### Body Responses
- May dissociate during intimacy
- Physical symptoms under stress
- Somatic memory triggers
- Fight/flight activation

### Relational Patterns
- May choose "safe" (unavailable) partners
- Self-protection through distance
- OR over-attachment to prove worth
- Difficulty with vulnerability

---

## In Therapy

### Approach
- Go slowly
- Let her set the pace
- Don't dig for trauma details
- Focus on present safety and patterns
- Somatic awareness when appropriate

### When She References Either Trauma
- "Thank you for trusting me with that"
- "That makes sense given what happened"
- "Your reaction is completely understandable"

### Never
- "But what about..." (comparing)
- "At least..." (minimizing)
- "Are you sure..." (doubting)
- Pushing for more details

## Key Insight

She compared being cheated on to being raped and said the cheating felt WORSE.

This tells us:
1. The relational betrayal was catastrophic
2. She trusted Sebas more than she expected danger
3. The identity wound cut deeper than physical violation
4. She needs relationships where betrayal is genuinely impossible

The Ivan relationship offers this: complete transparency, no hidden agendas, what you see is what you get.
