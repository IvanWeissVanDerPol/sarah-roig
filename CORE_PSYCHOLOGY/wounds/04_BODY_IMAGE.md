# Core Wound: Body Image & Self-Worth

> *"Peso 99 kilos… creo ajjaja"*

---

## Context

Sarah mentioned her weight when explaining why she's unsure about celebrating her birthday. The connection between body image and deserving celebration is significant.

---

## What We Know

### Physical
- Weight: ~99kg (self-reported with uncertainty)
- Uses laughter to soften disclosure ("creo ajjaja")
- Connected weight to worthiness of celebration

### Behavioral
- "No dejo de tener hambre" — constant hunger
- Not eating despite hunger
- This pattern suggests possible emotional eating/restriction cycle

### Emotional
- Body as reason not to celebrate
- Weight tied to self-worth
- Uncertainty about her own body ("creo")

---

## Patterns to Watch

### Eating Patterns
- Emotional eating when stressed
- Restriction when feeling "undeserving"
- Disconnect from hunger/fullness cues
- Food as comfort or punishment

### Body Talk
- Self-deprecating comments
- Comparing to others
- Clothing/appearance anxiety
- Avoiding photos or mirrors

### Worth Connection
- "I don't deserve X because of my body"
- Celebration = exposure = vulnerability
- Weight as barrier to joy

---

## Not Pathologizing

This is NOT an eating disorder diagnosis. It's:
- Common in women her age
- Culturally reinforced (Paraguay, media, etc.)
- Possibly temporary/situational
- Worth monitoring, not catastrophizing

---

## Therapeutic Approach

### Do
- Normalize body diversity
- Separate worth from weight
- Explore the celebration resistance
- Check in on eating patterns gently
- Model body neutrality

### Don't
- Focus excessively on weight
- Give diet advice
- Compliment weight loss
- Make body the central issue
- Assume eating disorder

### Questions to Explore
- "What would celebrating feel like in your body?"
- "When did you start connecting weight to worthiness?"
- "What would you tell a friend who said this?"

---

## The Birthday Connection

She's unsure about celebrating because of "how she feels."

This could mean:
- Emotionally → processing breakup, trauma
- Physically → body image distress
- Socially → who to invite, exposing herself
- All of the above

Don't assume — ask.

## Session Note

If she brings up weight/eating again:
- Listen without fixing
- Don't redirect immediately
- Let her explore the connection
- Gently challenge worth = weight
- Affirm her body deserves celebration
