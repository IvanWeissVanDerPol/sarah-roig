# Core Wound: "Not Enough"

> *"He chose her over me."*

---

## Origin

**December 2021** — Sebas left Sarah for Lisa (Austrian woman) and MARRIED her.

The message received:
- Another woman was better
- Sarah was replaceable
- Giving everything wasn't enough
- She was the practice run, not the destination

---

## The Specific Pain

It wasn't just abandonment. It was **replacement**.

> *"Hicimos el amor llorando y a los dos días te acostaste con ella"*

Days. Not months. Not a slow fade. DAYS between emotional intimacy with Sarah and sex with the woman he'd marry.

### What This Teaches Her (Falsely)
- Emotional connection doesn't protect you
- You can be completely intimate and still be left
- Someone else is always waiting to take your place
- Your best isn't good enough

---

## How It Manifests

### In Relationships
- Over-functioning to "earn" staying
- Constant comparison to other women
- Fear when partner mentions anyone female
- Need to be "perfect" to avoid replacement

### In Self-Image
- Questioning her worth
- "What does she have that I don't?"
- Feeling fundamentally lacking
- Imposter syndrome in love

### In Sexuality
- Using sex to secure attachment
- Fear that she's not satisfying enough
- Performing rather than enjoying
- Anxiety during intimacy

---

## The Truth (Reframes)

### His Choice ≠ Her Worth
Sebas's decision to pursue Lisa reflects:
- His inability to commit
- His pattern of deception
- His emotional immaturity
- HIS brokenness, not hers

### Being Left ≠ Being Lacking
She wasn't "not enough" — he was incapable of appreciating what he had.

### Evidence Against the Wound
Ivan's words: *"Para mí vos sos un 10/10. No hay nada que te falte como hombre."*

When she's with someone capable of seeing her, she's seen as **complete**.

---

## Therapeutic Work

### 1. Challenge the Narrative
- "What if the problem was his character, not your worth?"
- "Would you tell a friend she wasn't enough if this happened to her?"

### 2. Externalize the Wound
- "That's the 'Not Enough' story talking"
- Separate the wound from identity

### 3. Evidence Collection
Actively note moments that contradict the belief:
- Times she's been chosen
- Compliments received and believed
- Relationships where she WAS enough

### 4. Self-Compassion
- She was young (2021)
- She trusted someone who betrayed her
- That's not a character flaw; that's being human

---

## What She Actually Is

From analysis of her communications:

1. **Emotionally intelligent** — Articulates feelings precisely
2. **Loyal** — Commits fully to relationships
3. **Passionate** — Intense capacity for love
4. **Resilient** — Survived betrayal, still loves
5. **Authentic** — Genuine when she feels safe
6. **Sexually confident** — Knows and communicates her needs
7. **Funny** — Uses humor skillfully
8. **Deep** — Capable of profound connection

**She is more than enough. She was just with someone who couldn't count.**
