# Core Wound: Christmas Abandonment Pattern

> *"They always leave at Christmas."*

---

## The Pattern

| Year | Partner | What Happened |
|------|---------|---------------|
| 2021 | Sebas | Cheating revealed December 3rd, fully abandoned by Christmas |
| 2025-26 | NM | Relationship deteriorated around holidays |

**Two different people. Same timing. Same result.**

This isn't coincidence — it's now a trauma pattern.

---

## Why Christmas?

### Cultural Weight
- Family time = heightened vulnerability
- Expectations of love and togetherness
- Contrast between "should be" and "actually is"

### Emotional Exposure
- Already more emotionally open during holidays
- Less defended, more hopeful
- Betrayal hits harder when defenses are down

### Memory Consolidation
- Strong emotions + significant date = permanent memory
- Now the season itself is a trigger
- Body remembers before mind does

---

## Symptoms to Watch (Nov-Dec)

### Physiological
- Increased anxiety
- Sleep disruption
- Hypervigilance in relationships
- Physical tension

### Psychological
- Waiting for the other shoe to drop
- Interpreting neutral events as abandonment signs
- Preemptive emotional withdrawal
- Catastrophic thinking

### Behavioral
- May push partners away to "beat them to it"
- Over-giving to prevent abandonment
- Excessive reassurance-seeking
- Difficulty being present for holiday joy

---

## Therapeutic Approaches

### 1. Psychoeducation
Explain the pattern explicitly. Name it:
- "Your body learned that December = danger"
- "This is a trauma response, not reality testing"

### 2. Anticipatory Planning
Before November each year:
- Acknowledge the season is hard
- Increase support (therapy, friends)
- Have coping strategies ready
- Plan positive experiences to balance

### 3. Differentiation
Help separate THEN from NOW:
- "That was Sebas. This is [current situation]."
- "Your past doesn't determine your future"
- Different person ≠ same outcome

### 4. Somatic Work
Address the body-level response:
- Grounding exercises
- Breathing techniques
- Body awareness during trigger moments

### 5. Meaning-Making
Eventually (not immediately):
- Reclaim the season
- Create new associations
- Build positive holiday memories

---

## For Partners/Support System

### Do
- Be extra consistent Nov-Dec
- Explicitly reassure (without being asked)
- Follow through on commitments
- Name that you know this is a hard time

### Don't
- Make promises you can't keep
- Assume she's "over it"
- Take her anxiety personally
- Minimize the pattern

---

## Prognosis

This wound is **healable**. With:
- Awareness of the pattern
- Consistent positive experiences over time
- Supportive relationships
- Therapeutic processing

She can eventually experience December without dread.

---

*Note: Anniversary reactions are normal and expected. The goal isn't to eliminate the memory but to reduce its power over her present.*
