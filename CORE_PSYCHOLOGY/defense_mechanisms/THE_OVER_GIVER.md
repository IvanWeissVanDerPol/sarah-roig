# Defense Mechanism: The Over-Giver

> *"If I give enough, they'll stay."*

---

## Description

Excessive emotional, sexual, and practical investment in relationships, hoping it will prevent abandonment. Exhausts herself for partners who don't reciprocate.

---

## How It Manifests

### In Relationships
- Does more than her share emotionally
- Anticipates partner's needs before her own
- Says yes when she means no
- Keeps giving even when receiving nothing

### Sexually
- May use sex as attachment strategy
- Focuses on partner's pleasure over her own
- Fears being "not enough" in bed

### Emotionally
- Suppresses her needs to avoid being "demanding"
- Apologizes for having feelings
- Makes herself smaller to fit partner's capacity

---

## The Logic (And Why It Fails)

### The Belief
> "If I'm useful/giving/perfect enough, they won't leave."

### The Reality
- Sebas received everything → still left for Lisa
- Over-giving creates imbalance
- Attracts takers, not givers
- Breeds resentment (in both directions)
- She loses herself in the process

### The Truth
> You can't out-give abandonment. People who leave were going to leave regardless.

---

## Function

### What It Protects Against
- Abandonment (or so she hopes)
- Confronting that she can't control others
- Having to receive (which feels vulnerable)

### What It Costs
- Exhaustion
- Resentment
- Loss of self
- Attracting exploitative partners
- Never knowing if she's loved for HER or her labor

---

## In Therapy

### Questions to Ask
- "What would happen if you gave 50% instead of 150%?"
- "When did you learn that love requires earning?"
- "What are YOU getting from this relationship?"

### Reframes
- "Reciprocity isn't selfish, it's healthy."
- "You deserve someone who matches your effort."
- "Giving less doesn't mean you love less."

### Evidence
- With Ivan: She gives, but he MATCHES. 
- Result: She feels satisfied, not depleted.
- Proof: Reciprocity works better than over-giving.

---

## Healthy Evolution

From: **"I must earn love through labor."**  
To: **"I am loveable as I am. I give because I want to, and I receive because I deserve to."**

### The Goal
- Give from overflow, not depletion
- Match partner's energy level
- Receive without guilt
- Know she's worthy without proving it

---

## Origin Questions

- Who taught her love must be earned?
- Was she parentified as a child?
- Did she learn that her value = her usefulness?
- What happens in her body when she receives?
