# 12 Kilos (The Feeder's Anthem)

**Genre:** Alternative pop-punk / sad-girl acoustic with a bite  
**Vibe:** Olivia Rodrigo meets Julieta Venegas, but make it Paraguayan  
**Tone shift:** Innocent → broken → furious → hollow → free  

---

## Verse 1 — "El Salvador"

You said I was a lifeboat in the tempest of your days  
A chapter so transcendent, the whole world stopped to gaze  
*"El destino nos unió"* — you whispered it so sweet  
I didn't know that destinies came with a receipt  

Días pactados, ocho horas, "cargá la cámara"  
Te pienso tomar examen, ponete las pilas, mi alma  
And I said yes, because isn't that what love is?  
Changing yourself into the shape of what his fantasy is  

## Chorus — "12 Kilos"

Doce kilos for your love  
Doce kilos and it still wasn't enough  
You wanted all my body, all my time, all my shame  
And when I gave it all you said another woman's name  

Doce kilos that weren't mine  
They were yours, this weight, this shrine  
To a man who said he'd stay if I'd just change  
Doce kilos — baby, that's your change  

## Verse 2 — "El Deadline"

I was playing Roblox with a child when the clock struck twelve  
Trying to decide if I could live inside your hell  
And you gave me a deadline — midnight, not a minute late  
Como si el amor se rindiera en un examen de selectividad  

You said *"No retuviste el plazo que te indiqué"*  
Like I failed a class I didn't know I had to take  
And I said sorry, mea culpa, mea maxima culpa  
I said sorry so many times I forgot I had a pulse  

*sorry sorry sorry sorry —*  
The only Spanish you taught me was the language of remorse  

## Chorus — "12 Kilos"

Doce kilos for your love  
Doce kilos and it still wasn't enough  
You wanted all my body, all my time, all my shame  
And when I gave it all you said another woman's name  

## Bridge — "The Mirror"

*"Seguí tomando té todo light"* — your final gift to me  
After twelve kilos for your fetish, you're giving diet tips for free?  

I looked in the mirror and I didn't know the girl who stared back  
99 kilos of accommodation, not an ounce of her own back  
*"Me odio, me detesto, la felicidad no es lo mío"*  
I whispered at 4 AM while you slept like you had nothing to lose  

But those words weren't mine — they were yours injected in my skin  
Twelve kilos of evidence of where your love had been  
And I couldn't see it, couldn't feel it, couldn't name it then  
Because I was still saying *"nunca podré agradecerte"*  
Still saying thank you to the man who took my shape and bent it  

## Verse 3 — "The Reclamation"

Ay, Ianna tiene brillo en los ojos, me dijiste  
Y yo tenía veinte minutos de retraso y vos te desviviste  
For the drama, for the script, for the role you cast me in  
La payasa patética, la gorda, la que siempre pierde al fin  

Pero sabés qué? Ya no tengo hambre  
No de tu amor, no de tu pan, no de tus reglas de examen  
*"No estoy para ser tu compañero, no me da el tiempo ni la paciencia"*  
Y yo ya no estoy para ser tu proyecto, tu gimnasio, tu paciencia  

## Final Chorus — "Los Kilos Vuelven a Casa"

Esos doce kilos no eran míos — eran tuyos  
Eran el peso de tu voz diciendo "cambiá, cambiá, cambiá"  
Eran las horas muerta en tu casa esperando que volvás  
Eran las veces que dije "sí" cuando quería gritar "no"  

Y ahora los devuelvo  
No en peso, no en llanto, no en una canción de tres minutos  
Los devuelvo en silencio  
En el acto de no responder más tus mensajes a las 4 AM  

Doce kilos se fueron con vos  
Y yo me quedo con los huesos  
Y con la certeza de que el amor  
No se mide en kilos  

## Outro — Spoken

Qué poético.

Lástima que el salvavidas estaba lleno de agujeros  
Y el naufragio eras vos

## Production Notes

- **Verse 1:** Soft, breathy. Almost romantic. Ukulele or clean guitar. She still believes.
- **Chorus:** Drums kick in. Voice gains edge. The first "doce kilos" is almost an accusation.
- **Verse 2:** Spoken-word adjacent. The "deadline" delivery should be flat — comedic bitterness.
- **Bridge:** Stripped down. The mirror moment. Let the Sarah quotes land raw.
- **Outro:** Spoken over a single guitar note ringing out. The last line is a mic drop.

**Runtime:** ~3:30  
**Key:** Em → G → C → Am (sad-girl classic)  
**Suggested influence:** "Drivers License" structure + "Good 4 U" energy
