## What Helps

### DO ✅

- **Be consistent** — Show up when you say you will
- **Reassure explicitly** — "I'm not going anywhere" actually helps
- **Match her energy** — She's intense; that's not a flaw
- **Let her vent** — Sometimes she just needs to talk it out
- **Use humor** — She processes through jokes; laugh with her
- **Physical presence** — Hugs, touch, being there
- **Follow through** — Broken promises confirm her fears

### DON'T ❌

- **Don't disappear** — Even short silences can trigger anxiety
- **Don't minimize** — "Just get over it" doesn't work
- **Don't compare** — Her pain is her pain
- **Don't push solutions** — Listen first, fix later (if asked)
- **Don't make promises you can't keep** — Better to under-promise
- **Don't bring up exes casually** — She's still processing

---

## When She's Anxious

### Signs
- Rapid texting
- Asking "are you okay?" repeatedly
- Checking if you're mad
- Overthinking your responses

### Response
1. Acknowledge: "I see you're worried"
2. Reassure: "Everything's fine between us"
3. Be present: Don't dismiss, stay engaged
4. Physical if possible: A hug does more than words

## Ivan's Role (Specific)

You represent something important: **Proof that healthy love exists.**

- She trusts you completely (rare for her)
- You can be the reference point: "This is how it should feel"
- Your consistency heals her inconsistent past
- Don't overcorrect — just keep being you

**One ask:** If you need space, SAY IT. Don't just go quiet. Silence = abandonment to her brain.

## When to Escalate

### Red flags to watch
- Stops eating for extended periods
- Talks about not wanting to be here
- Complete withdrawal from everyone
- Self-harm mentions

### What to do
1. Take it seriously
2. Stay with her (don't leave her alone)
3. Contact Nyx/Ivan
4. Crisis resources if needed

---

## What NOT to Fix

You're support, not therapist. Don't try to:
- Resolve her trauma
- Make her "get over" the exes
- Analyze her patterns (that's Nyx's job)
- Push her to forgive anyone

Just be present. That's enough.
